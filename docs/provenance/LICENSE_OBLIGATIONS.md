# License, Notice and SBOM Obligations

Source transparency and legal permission are separate checks.

For every admitted upstream:

1. pin the exact revision;
2. read the exact LICENSE and NOTICE files at that revision;
3. record the SPDX expression and LICENSE SHA-256;
4. preserve required copyright and notice text;
5. classify source-disclosure, attribution and patent clauses;
6. verify compatibility with ActionGate's intended distribution mode;
7. create or update an exact-subject SPDX SBOM;
8. retain a replacement or removal path.

`MIT`, `BSD`, `Apache-2.0` or another permissive label is not by itself employer-IP, patent, trademark or legal clearance.
